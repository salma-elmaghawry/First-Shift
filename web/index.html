Where it breaks

Graduates have projects. Few can explain the decisions behind them.

  1. Week 12Course completed. Certificate issued.
  2. Week 13Portfolio project deployed. README written.
  3. Interview · 10:40“Why did you build it this way?”

  • Projects don’t prove understanding.

    Templates, tutorials and AI make finished work easy to produce. The reasoning is what’s missing.

  • Deep review doesn’t scale.

    Instructors can’t sit with every learner, on every project, every cohort.

  • Decisions never get practiced.

    Courses teach how to build. Very few make learners argue for a trade-off out loud.

  • Coaching time gets spread thin.

    Without evidence, a mentor spends half the session finding the problem.

Why not ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a tool. FirstShift is a program.

What the report is, and what it isn’t.

It is

  • A record of how one learner handled one task.
  • Findings linked to the submission and the defense transcript.
  • Checked by a human reviewer before it’s final.
  • Shown with the learner’s own note on how they used AI.

It isn’t

  • A hiring certification.
  • Proof that the work was done without help.
  • A percentage, a pass mark or an XP total.
  • A judgment of the person.

Practice the work. Defend your decisions. Know what to improve.

FS-101 Frontend · Payments · Bazaar storefront Up to 450 XP

Checkout says “Payment successful” before the server knows.

Bazaar is a small online store that takes card payments through Paymob. After paying, the customer lands on /checkout/return?success=true. The app reads that query string, shows the success screen and clears the cart. Support is getting tickets from customers whose cards were declined but who saw “Payment successful”.

CheckoutReturn.tsxcurrent
10 const params = new URLSearchParams(location.search);
11 if (params.get("success") === "true") {
12 clearCart();
13 return <SuccessScreen />;
14 }

Your task

  1. Stop treating the browser redirect as proof of payment.
  2. Show success only after the server confirms the order is paid.
  3. Design what the customer sees when the payment is pending, failed, or still unknown.
  4. Write a short test plan for the cases you think matter.

Stuck? Open a hint

0 of 3 opened

Hints are free and never appear in your report. Try the task for a few minutes first.

Hint 1 · What does the redirect prove?

Only that the customer’s browser came back to your page. Who else could open ?success=true? Could a declined card still redirect?

Hint 2 · Who knows the truth?

Paymob tells your server through a webhook. The page should ask your server for the order’s status, not decide on its own.

Hint 3 · What if the answer is late?

The webhook can take seconds, or never arrive. What does the customer see meanwhile, and what stops them paying twice?
